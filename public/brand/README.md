# Noria brand assets

Two opposing scoops suggest the turning wheel behind the Noria name. Their open center frames a range, connecting fluid movement with inspectable boundaries. Lime marks one side and teal marks the other; neither direction implies a price forecast.

The lowercase wordmark uses custom geometric paths, with rounded bowls and an open shoulder. Every asset is self-contained SVG, with no font or external resource dependencies.

- `noria-mark.svg`: transparent symbol, with a 64 × 64 viewBox.
- `noria-wordmark.svg`: dark wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `noria-wordmark-inverse.svg`: light wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `noria-lockup.svg`: mark and wordmark on a dark panel, ready for a README or presentation.

Palette: dark `#0D1210`, lime `#D3F78B`, teal `#78D9C1`, light `#EEF3EC`. Preserve the open gaps in the mark and leave at least one scoop-width of clear space around it. The app favicon uses the same mark on a rounded dark tile.
